Services

What we fix.

Every pair gets a careful inspection and the right repair, not a quick patch.

  • Hinge Repair

    Loose, wobbly or snapped hinges, including spring hinges, repaired or replaced.

  • Frame Soldering

    Broken bridges and snapped metal parts, rejoined and refinished to blend in.

  • Temple (Arm) Repair

    Bent, cracked or broken arms repaired, or replaced when they can't be saved.

  • Straightening & Alignment

    Frames that got sat on or twisted, brought back into shape so they sit level.

  • Nose Pads & Pad Arms

    Worn or broken nose pads and pad arms replaced for a comfortable fit.

  • Full Tune-Up

    New screws, tightened joints, cleaning and adjustment for a well-loved pair.

Not sure what your frames need? Describe the problem on your repair slip and we'll email you before doing any work.
