Mail-in repairs

How to send us your frames.

We work entirely by mail. Follow these steps and your glasses will be on their way back to you, repaired, before you know it.

  1. Fill out a repair slip

    Print the slip below (or write the same details on a sheet of paper) and include it in your package. Without it, we can't match your glasses to you.

  2. Pack them safely

    Put your glasses in a hard case, wrap the case in bubble wrap or tissue, and use a padded mailer or small box. Please don't send glasses in a plain envelope.

  3. Ship to our shop

    Mail your package to the address on this page. We recommend a shipping method with tracking so you can follow it.

  4. We inspect & confirm

    When your frames arrive, we inspect them and email you to confirm the repair and cost before we begin.

  5. Repaired & returned

    Once the work is done and paid for, we ship your frames back to the return address on your slip.
